Proposal for the four simulations · second draft, 24 October 2026

Same paper, different inks

The four sims share one ground, one typeface, one corner mark and one caption line. Everything else each takes from its own subject: a spacetime you can tilt for Worldline, a molecular painting over a transit map for Lumen, an epidemiological atlas for Miasma, and a trading desk's morning paper for Shoals. Every figure below is a live sketch running in your browser. This draft follows your notes on the first.
