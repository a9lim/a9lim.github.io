Shoals the Brief

Shoals already has a house paper. The Meridian Brief is the firm's morning note, and the event files quote it. So the interface becomes the Brief. Each sim day is an edition. Prices are set like a newspaper's agate tables. Event popups arrive as dispatches, and the epilogue is the last edition.

What the coupling teaches

A certification moves everything: the stock, vol, the compute curve into backwardation, and every later rung's timeline. A Fed hike moves the money markets and leaves the binaries where they were. A blockade scare lifts the far end of the compute curve most and pushes the timelines out. The sandbox makes the same point from the other side: a compute future and a milestone binary are two prices for one bet.
