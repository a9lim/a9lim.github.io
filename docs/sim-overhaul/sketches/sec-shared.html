What happens to shared/

Keep a shared skeleton and give each sim its own skin. Going fully standalone would copy the keyboard, touch, focus and dropdown accessibility code into four repositories without changing a pixel. The uniform look comes from base.css and the toolbar-and-sidebar layout, so those are what change.

FileFateWhy
shortcuts touch haptics utils cameraKeepBehaviour with no look of its own.
dropdown forms tabs tooltipKeep the logic, drop the stylingEach sim styles its own controls to its medium.
about infoKeepThe about panel is part of the shared colophon.
toolbarBecomes colophonSeal, name, play and step, theme, help. Each sim places everything else.
base.cssSplitThe shared part keeps the reset, focus, accessibility, colophon, caption and about panel. HUD surfaces move into each sim.
tokensShrinks, and takes the inksGround, text, accent, type and the eleven inks stay shared. The old extended keys are renamed in one pass across the four projects, with no aliases left behind. Each sim's colors.js maps its states to inks.
sparklineMoves into the simsEach medium draws its own small charts.
