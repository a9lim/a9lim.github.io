Decisions for you

Which pilot?

Worldline has the most momentum and the biggest single change. Miasma has the simplest renderer and is now the most finished sketch.

Does the shared ground hold everywhere?

All four stay on the site's neutrals. The atlas could take a paper tint, at the cost of the thread between them.

What does the red mean?

Here it marks the thing to watch: your body, the protons, the disease, losses. That moves your own marks in Miasma, the painted interventions, from red to ink.

Miasma's Klein bottle

The header comment in topology.js says Klein glues all three edge pairs, while wrapKlein leaves the third pair open. The sketch follows the code. Which is intended?
