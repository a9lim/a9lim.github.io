Trading simulator with GBM, Merton jumps, Heston stochastic volatility, and Vasicek rates driving a CRR binomial tree for American option pricing with discrete dividends. Has a 25-strike options chain, a multi-leg strategy builder with payoff diagrams and Greek overlays, a margin portfolio, and a narrative event engine with over 400 curated scenarios.
