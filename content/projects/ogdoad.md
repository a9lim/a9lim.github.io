A Rust engine for Clifford algebras and quadratic-form classification, built over the commutative number systems adjacent to Conway’s combinatorial games — which under disjunctive sum form an abelian group, not a scalar ring, so the algebras live over the field-like cores beside game theory rather than the games themselves. Those scalars form one table on two axes: place (Archimedean, transfinite, p-adic, function-field, finite — each field paired with its ring of integers) and characteristic (0, odd, 2). The same structures recur cell to cell with place and characteristic swapped: surreals recover the real Cl(p,q) classification with infinite and infinitesimal metric entries, characteristic-2 legs like the nimbers trade the 8-fold periodicity for the Arf invariant and Brauer–Wall group, and nilpotents let a degenerate form collapse to the full exterior algebra. Verified Rust core with Python bindings.
