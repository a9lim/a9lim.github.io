Semi-realistic nuclear reactor simulator covering PWR, RBMK-1000, and molten-salt reactor types on a shared physics engine: 1D axial point-kinetics with six delayed-neutron precursor groups, lumped fuel/clad/coolant thermal-hydraulics, the iodine-xenon chain, burnup-dependent feedback, and ANS-5.1 decay heat. Models the secondary plant — dynamic pressurizer, steam generators, ECCS, feedwater, turbine — plus a reactor-protection and annunciator network, an auto-regulating rod controller, natural-circulation regimes, and the RBMK positive-void and graphite-tip effects behind Chernobyl.
