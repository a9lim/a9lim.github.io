\section*{Selected Projects}

\role{\href{https://github.com/a9lim/saklas}{Saklas}}{PyPI \textperiodcentered{} Python}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Activation steering and trait monitoring for HuggingFace transformers --- extracts contrastive steering vectors and adds them to hidden states at generation time, no fine-tuning required.
  \item Three interfaces: a terminal UI with live alpha knobs and probe sparklines, an HTTP server speaking both OpenAI \texttt{/v1/*} and Ollama \texttt{/api/*} wire formats on the same port, and a Python API for scripted experiments.
  \item Ships 21 pre-built probes scoring affect, epistemic stance, register, and alignment in-flight; tested on Qwen, Gemma, Ministral, gpt-oss, Llama, and GLM.
  \item Implements the contrastive-PCA reading procedure from \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01405}{Zou et al.\ (2023)}; published to PyPI under AGPL-3.0 with CI, type checking, and llama.cpp GGUF interchange.
\end{itemize}

\role{\href{https://a9l.im/geon}{Geon} --- Relativistic Particle Physics}{JavaScript \textperiodcentered{} WebGPU}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Real-time N-body simulator running on WebGPU compute shaders, modeling 11 force types --- Newtonian gravity, gravitomagnetism, Coulomb, Lorentz, Yukawa, Higgs and axion field couplings, Hubble expansion, 1PN general-relativistic corrections, spin-orbit, and radiation reaction.
  \item Barnes-Hut tree acceleration for $O(N \log N)$ scaling; Boris integrator preserving phase-space volume.
  \item Black-hole mode with Kerr-Newman event horizons, Hawking radiation, Schwinger pair-production discharge, and superradiant axion clouds. Nineteen curated presets demonstrate Keplerian orbits, Rutherford scattering, Higgs wells, gravitational-wave inspiral, and more.
\end{itemize}

\role{\href{https://a9l.im/cyano}{Cyano} --- Cellular Metabolism}{JavaScript}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Interactive biochemistry simulator covering twelve metabolic pathways --- glycolysis, gluconeogenesis, PPP, Krebs, beta-oxidation, fatty acid synthesis, the Calvin cycle, the light reactions, fermentation, the urea cycle, and amino acid catabolism --- connected through shared metabolite pools.
  \item 14-complex electron transport chain with proton motive force, oxidative phosphorylation, uncoupling, leak, and reactive oxygen species generation; allosteric regulation gates every reaction (PFK, PDH, ICDH).
  \item Six organism presets including a cancer-cell preset that demonstrates the Warburg effect.
\end{itemize}

\role{\href{https://a9l.im/shoals}{Shoals} --- Options Trading}{JavaScript}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Derivatives pricing simulator combining Heston stochastic volatility and Merton jump diffusion with a Vasicek mean-reverting interest rate. American options priced via 128-step Cox-Ross-Rubinstein binomial tree with term-structure volatility, moneyness skew, and discrete dividends.
  \item 25-strike options chain with real-time Greeks, multi-leg strategy builder (spreads, straddles, condors, butterflies), payoff diagrams, and portfolio-level margin tracking.
  \item Narrative event engine with 400+ curated scenarios --- earnings, monetary policy, geopolitics, sector rotation, technical signals, black swans --- chained via a Poisson scheduler with trait-aware likelihood weighting.
\end{itemize}

\role{\href{https://a9l.im/gerry}{Gerry} --- Redistricting \& Electoral Fairness}{JavaScript}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Interactive gerrymandering simulator on a procedural hex-tile electorate. Players paint districts and evaluate them against six fairness metrics: efficiency gap, partisan symmetry, competitive-district count, Polsby-Popper compactness, contiguity, and majority-minority districts.
  \item Automated modes include pack-and-crack and a simulated-annealing fair-draw optimizer; Monte Carlo election stress tests run thousands of simulated elections with turnout noise to evaluate map robustness.
  \item Procedural maps generated via seeded Perlin noise with configurable urban clustering and minority density, reproducible by URL hash.
\end{itemize}

\role{\href{https://a9l.im/scripture/}{Scripture} --- Sacred Text Reader}{JavaScript}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Browser-based reader for sixteen sacred texts spanning Christian, Islamic, LDS, Confucian, Taoist, Shinto, Zoroastrian, Buddhist, Finnish, and Norse traditions --- $\sim$50\,MB of static JSON, loaded on demand per chapter.
  \item Full-text search across all sixteen works, TF-IDF concordance for related passage discovery, verse-linked notes, text-to-speech, and deep linking to any verse via URL.
  \item Edge-SSR'd verse content with per-chapter \texttt{Chapter} JSON-LD and per-verse \texttt{Quotation} structured data so the corpus is crawlable without JavaScript execution.
\end{itemize}
